### MVP

A multiplayer or simulated spatial scene where each avatar has a daemon that can:

* follow the avatar
* display one of 3–5 social states
* display one of 5–8 selected topics/interests
* visually transform based on state/topic
* detect nearby compatible users
* merge or amplify signals when users cluster

* Open to chat
* Looking for collaborators
* Deep in focus
* Just browsing
* Ask me about X

### Example topics

* AI agents
* astronomy
* music
* game design
* accessibility
* worldbuilding

### Example visible outputs

* animal form changes
* aura or color shift
* icon/banner above avatar
* merged beacon when two or more similar topics gather
