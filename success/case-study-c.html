Health Scoring & Renewal Confidence

Building a resilient "Production OS" for high-stakes environments.

Situation

TEDxAsheville is a non-profit organization run entirely by volunteers. We produce high-stakes live events for 1200+ attendees. The challenge: 100% potential team turnover every year, yet the event must be perfect.

Obstacles

  • Fragility: Key institutional knowledge was trapped in people's heads.
  • High Stakes: Live production allows zero errors. One mistake breaks trust with the audience.
  • Resource Constraint: $0 budget for staff; relied on "emotional paycheck" (volunteers).

Actions

  • "Production OS": Codified every role into a playbook. Checklists replaced memory.
  • Health Signals: Established "Green/Yellow/Red" status checks at weekly standups. If a department was Red, we swarmed to help.
  • Post-Event QBRs: After every event, we held a ruthless (but kind) retrospective to update the playbooks.

Results

  • 14 Years of Sold-Out Events: Consistent quality built a massive brand trust in the community.
  • Zero Critical Failures: The system caught errors before they went live.
  • Renewal: Volunteers returned year after year because they felt supported and successful.

What I'd Repeat

Clarity is Kind. In high-stress renewals or escalations, the most empathetic thing you can do is provide a clear process. Structure creates safety.
