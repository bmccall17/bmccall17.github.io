Adoption & Enablement Program

Using spatial context to drive behavior change in remote teams.

Situation

Mural XR aimed to bring agile project planning into a spatial computing environment. Remote teams were struggling with "flat" 2D tools (Jira, Trello) during complex planning sessions, leading to low engagement and information recall.

Obstacles

  • Behavioral Inertia: Users were used to 2D lists and resistant to "3D" interfaces that felt gimmicky.
  • Hardware Friction: WebXR performance constraints meant the experience had to be lightweight yet compelling.
  • Adoption Gap: Getting a team to switch tools for a critical meeting is high risk.

Actions

  • Spatial "Project Canvas": Designed a timeline that users could physically "walk" along. Distance = Time.
  • Low-Friction Onboarding: Removed login walls for guest participants; "One-click to join" from Slack.
  • Visual cues: Used height and color to indicate task complexity and status, making the "health" of the project visible at a glance.

Results

  • 40% Increase in Repeat Engagement: Teams returned because the spatial memory aids helped them recall decisions better.
  • "Aha!" Moments: Users reported feeling "inside the data" rather than just looking at it.
  • Champion Enablement: Scrum Masters became internal champions because it made their standups more engaging.

What I'd Repeat

Spatial Context as Enablement. When users can "see" the problem physically, they adopt the solution faster. Enablement isn't just training manuals; it's intuitive design.
