Products & Technology

Our platform processes 1.2 petabytes of data annually from thousands of sources. We deploy 12,600 scouts worldwide, collecting live data across 35 data applications. Our Computer Vision technology captures data at 120 frames per second — 100x more data than a human scout.

Product What It Does
4Sight Streaming AI/ML/computer vision overlays on live streams. 3.6M data points per Bundesliga match. 240 additional live betting markets. Viewers spend 24% longer on 4Sight streams.
GameFrame AI transforms live player-tracking data into on-air graphics, animated replays, shot charts. Powers NBC Sports RSN NBA broadcasts.
Alpha Odds ML models processing 250 in-play markets per match for dynamic pricing.
Computer Vision 120 fps live tracking. Powers player tracking, animated replays, shot charts across multiple sports.
Integrity Exchange Global bet monitoring and match-fixing detection. 1M+ events monitored, UFDS AI detection system.
Playradar New iGaming sub-brand (Mar 2026) combining sports data expertise with casino content for global operators.
